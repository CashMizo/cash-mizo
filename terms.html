Legal & Privacy Center

Transparency, Security, and Trust for our Users

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

At CASH-MIZO, we prioritize your digital privacy. To provide a seamless and secure experience, we only collect essential information required to create and maintain your account. When you register, we securely process your Name and Email Address via Google OAuth.

We may also collect non-personal identification information about users whenever they interact with our app. This includes device models, operating systems, and IP addresses, which are standard metrics used to ensure the platform operates smoothly and securely across all devices.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for account identification, reward tracking, and payout processing. We use your email to send important updates regarding your withdrawals or account status.

Your linked payment details (such as UPI ID, PayPal Email, or Bank Account numbers) are used exclusively by our automated system to disburse your earned rewards safely. We do not use your personal data for targeted advertising, nor do we run background tracking software.

3. Data Protection & Security

We adopt appropriate data collection, storage, and processing practices alongside industry-standard security protocols to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information.

Your sensitive data is fully encrypted. We guarantee that your information is never leased, sold, traded, or shared with unauthorized third-party marketing agencies or external entities.

Terms of Service

1. Account Integrity & Usage

By using the CASH-MIZO platform, you agree to maintain fair play and respect the community guidelines. We strictly enforce a "One Device, One Account" policy to ensure a balanced economy within the app.

Attempting to create multiple user profiles on a single smartphone to exploit the referral system, daily rewards, or surveys is strictly prohibited. Users found violating this rule will have all associated accounts terminated and earnings forfeited.

2. Anti-Fraud & VPN Policy

Our platform relies on verified, location-based tasks provided by our partners. To maintain the trust of our advertisers and ensure the longevity of our platform, any fraudulent activity is met with zero tolerance.

The use of the following tools or methods will result in immediate, permanent account suspension without prior notice:

  • Virtual Private Networks (VPNs), Proxy servers, or VPS to spoof geographical locations.
  • Emulators (e.g., Bluestacks, Nox) or automated clicker scripts/bots.
  • Modded APKs, reverse engineering, or any attempt to alter the application's client code.
