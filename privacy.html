Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal data.

Complete Privacy Guidelines

1. Information We Collect

At CASH-MIZO, we believe in minimal data collection. To provide a seamless and secure experience, we only collect essential information required to create and maintain your account. When you register with us, we securely process your Name and Email Address via Google OAuth.

To ensure our platform runs smoothly and to prevent fraudulent activities, our automated systems may collect non-personal identification data. This includes your device model, operating system version, generic geographical region, and IP address. This data is purely analytical and cannot be used to personally identify you outside of our system.

2. How We Use Your Information

We do not collect data just for the sake of it. Your data is strictly used for the following operational purposes:

  • Account Identification: To track your unique rewards, daily tasks, and leaderboard rankings.
  • Payout Processing: Your linked payment details (such as UPI ID, PayPal Email, or Bank Account numbers) are used exclusively by our automated system to disburse your earned rewards safely.
  • Communication: We use your email to send important updates regarding your account status, withdrawal confirmations, or policy changes.

3. Data Protection & Security

We adopt strict data collection, storage, and processing practices alongside industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information.

Your sensitive data is fully encrypted within our secure database hosted on robust servers. We guarantee that your information is never leased, sold, traded, or shared with unauthorized third-party marketing agencies.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Partners

Your trust is our priority. We only share generic, anonymized data with verified partners (like Offerwalls and Survey providers) solely to verify task completions and credit coins to your account.

In the event of a legal request from government authorities, we will comply only if it is mandated by the law to protect the platform against severe fraud or cybercrimes.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our app uses basic "cookies" to enhance User experience. A cookie is a small text file placed on your device to remember your session, preferences, and login state so you don't have to log in repeatedly.

We do not use tracking cookies to follow your activity across other websites or apps. You can choose to set your device to refuse cookies, but doing so may cause some parts of the App (like automatic logins) to function improperly.

6. Children's Privacy

CASH-MIZO is intended for users who are at least 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13 without verifiable parental consent.

If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will take immediate steps to delete that information from our servers.

7. User Rights & Data Control

You are in complete control of your data. Under applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct any inaccurate payment information from your Profile section.
  • Restriction: Opt-out of certain data collection processes, though this may limit your ability to earn rewards.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with our services. If you remain inactive for a consecutive period of 12 months, your account and all associated data may be scheduled for permanent deletion to optimize server resources.

You have the full right to request the deletion of your account at any time. Simply contact our support team, and all your personal credentials, linked payment methods, and coin balances will be permanently erased from our database within 15 business days.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

CASH-MIZO has the discretion to update this privacy policy at any time. When we do, we will revise the updated date at the bottom of this page and send an in-app notification.

We encourage Users to frequently check this page for any changes to stay informed about how we are helping to protect the personal information we collect. Your continued use of the App following the posting of changes to this policy will be deemed your acceptance of those changes.
